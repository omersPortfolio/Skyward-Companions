Skyward: The Companions is a 3D mobile platformer where players ascend through a series of challenging levels, each themed around growth, perseverance, and cooperation. With each episode, players follow the journey of unique characters as they overcome obstacles and climb ever higher.

- Vertical platforming gameplay with narrative progression
- Episodic structure with unique challenges in each chapter
